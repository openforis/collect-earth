If macOS says the installer cannot be opened

The installer is not yet notarized by Apple, so the first time macOS blocks it. To open it:

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and double-click the installer. When macOS says it cannot be opened, click Done (or OK).
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to the message about Collect Earth.
  3. Confirm with Open Anyway and your password. From then on it opens normally.

On macOS 14 and older you can also right-click (Control-click) the installer, choose Open, then Open again.

Advanced users can instead clear the download mark in Terminal before opening the .dmg:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/collect-earth-installer-PROJECT_VERSION-osx.dmg
