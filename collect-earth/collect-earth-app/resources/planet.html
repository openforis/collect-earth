A Planet key is needed

Planet's mosaics are shown with your own Planet key, one that gives access to the Tropical Forest Observatory.

In Collect Earth, enter it in Settings › Imagery › Planet API key, with Planet imagery set to “Monthly mosaics (Tropical Forest Observatory)”, then open the plot again.

Since the NICFI programme ended in January 2025, Collect Earth no longer provides a key of its own.
