Try the Collect Earth 2 beta

Collect Earth 2 works inside Google Earth Web, in your browser, instead of Google Earth Desktop. It is the version these guides describe, and it is ready to try. Install it, use it on your own plots and tell us what works and what doesn't.

What is new

  • Inside Google Earth Web. The plot list and the form are a panel in the Google Earth Web page, which flies to each plot and draws it on the imagery. Google Earth Desktop is no longer needed.
  • A form made from your survey. Steps with Back, Next and Submit, filled from the keyboard as fast as you can answer. Same as previous plot (P) copies the choices of the last plot you submitted, marked to be checked.
  • All the imagery in one place. ESRI Wayback, Planet, Earth Engine, Earth Map and Street View open in one browser window, a tab per source, and Collect Earth lays the windows out on one or two screens by itself.
  • Cloud projects. Join a project on Collect Earth Cloud or on your own server, and every plot you save reaches the team's dashboard by itself (see Which set-up?).
  • Its own Java 21. The installer brings everything Collect Earth needs, so there is nothing else to install apart from a browser.

Before you install

  • A 64-bit computer with Windows, macOS or Linux. Macs with an Intel or an Apple chip both work.
  • Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  • An internet connection, all the time you work: the imagery comes from Google Earth Web and the other imagery services.
  • A download of about 250 MB. You don't need administrator rights.
It is a beta. Collect Earth 2 is still being tested. If Collect Earth 1 is on this computer, the beta installs in its place and opens your projects and data as they are. Before you install it, export your data (Data Import/Export, as compressed XML) and keep the file somewhere safe. For work with a deadline, you can stay on Collect Earth 1 until Collect Earth 2 is final.

Check the download (optional)

Each release has a file SHA256SUMS with the fingerprint of every installer, and SHA256SUMS.asc, its signature by Open Foris. Download SHA256SUMS next to the installer, open a terminal in that folder and compare the installer's fingerprint with its line in the file.

Windows, in PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\collect-earth-installer-*-windows.exe

macOS:

shasum -a 256 collect-earth-installer-*-osx.dmg

Linux, which compares them for you and shows OK:

sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing

With GPG, download SHA256SUMS.asc too: gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.asc SHA256SUMS checks that the list itself comes from Open Foris.

Install

Windows

  1. Double-click the .exe you downloaded.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose your language, accept the licence and keep the folder it proposes, C:\OpenForis\CollectEarth.
  4. When it finishes, start Collect Earth from the icon on your desktop or from the Start menu.

macOS

  1. Double-click the .dmg you downloaded, then the installer inside it.
  2. If macOS says it cannot check the installer, click Done (or OK). Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about the Collect Earth installer, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password.
  3. Follow the steps. Collect Earth goes into OpenForis/CollectEarth in your home folder, with a Collect Earth icon on your desktop.
  4. Start Collect Earth from that icon. On a Mac with an Apple chip, macOS may ask to install Rosetta the first time: click Install.

Linux

  1. Open a terminal in the folder you downloaded the installer to, make it executable and run it: 
    chmod +x collect-earth-installer-*-linux-x64.run
./collect-earth-installer-*-linux-x64.run
  2. Follow the steps. You don't need sudo: Collect Earth goes into ~/OpenForis/CollectEarth, with a launcher on your desktop.
  3. Start Collect Earth from that launcher (if your desktop asks, right-click it and choose Allow Launching), or run ~/OpenForis/CollectEarth/CollectEarth-launcher.run.

First start

  1. A small window shows each step while Collect Earth opens. The first start can take up to a minute.
  2. Collect Earth opens in your browser, inside Google Earth Web, with a demo project to try out. Google may ask you to accept its cookies or to sign in in that window; Collect Earth remembers it afterwards.
  3. Type your name when asked. It is saved with every plot you assess.

Collect Earth keeps running in the system tray (the menu bar on a Mac). From here, the guide for interpreters takes over: opening your project, assessing plots and joining a cloud project. Coordinators, see Running a cloud project.

Updates, going back and removing it

A new beta. Install it over the one you have, in the same way. Your projects, data and settings stay.

Your data. Collect Earth keeps your projects and plots in a folder of their own, apart from the program. Removing the program leaves that folder on your computer until you delete it yourself.

SystemYour data folder
Windows%APPDATA%\CollectEarth
macOS~/Library/Application Support/CollectEarth
Linux~/CollectEarth

Going back to Collect Earth 1. Install it again from the Open Foris download page. If it does not open your project as before, import the data you exported before installing the beta.

Removing it. On Windows, open Settings › Apps › Installed apps, then Collect Earth › Uninstall, or choose Uninstall Collect Earth in the Start menu. On macOS and Linux, run the uninstall program in OpenForis/CollectEarth in your home folder.
