Try the Collect Earth 2 beta

Collect Earth 2 is a program you install on your computer. It works inside Google Earth Web, in your browser, instead of Google Earth Desktop. It is the version these guides describe, and it is ready to try. Install it, use it on your own plots and tell us what works and what doesn't.

What is new

  • Inside Google Earth Web. The plot list and the form are a panel in the Google Earth Web page, which flies to each plot and draws it on the imagery. Google Earth Desktop (Google Earth Pro) is no longer used.
  • A form made from your survey. Steps with Back, Next and Submit, filled from the keyboard as fast as you can answer. Same as previous plot (P) copies the choices of the last plot you submitted, marked to be checked.
  • All the imagery in one place. ESRI Wayback, Planet, Collect Earth Explorer (Earth Engine imagery, with charts of each plot over the years), Earth Map and Street View open in one browser window, a tab per source, and Collect Earth lays the windows out on one or two screens by itself.
  • Larger plot files. Up to 40,000 plots in a file, where Collect Earth 1 warned past 2,000.
  • Cloud projects. Join a project on Collect Earth Cloud or on your own server: every plot you save reaches the team's dashboard by itself, and the plots your coordinator gives you reach your Collect Earth by themselves (see Which set-up?).
  • Its own Java 21. The installer brings everything Collect Earth needs, so there is nothing else to install apart from a browser.

Before you install

  • A 64-bit computer with Windows, macOS or Linux. Macs with an Intel or an Apple chip both work.
  • Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  • An internet connection, all the time you work: the imagery comes from Google Earth Web and the other imagery services.
  • A download of about 250 MB. You don't need administrator rights.
It is a beta. Collect Earth 2 is still being tested. If Collect Earth 1 is on this computer, the beta installs in its place and opens your projects and data as they are. Before you install it, export your data (Data Import/Export, as compressed XML) and keep the file somewhere safe. For work with a deadline, you can stay on Collect Earth 1 until Collect Earth 2 is final.

Check the download (optional)

Each release has a file SHA256SUMS with the fingerprint of every installer, and SHA256SUMS.asc, its signature by Open Foris. Download SHA256SUMS next to the installer, open a terminal in that folder and compare the installer's fingerprint with its line in the file.

Windows, in PowerShell:

Get-FileHash .\collect-earth-installer-*-windows.exe

macOS:

shasum -a 256 collect-earth-installer-*-osx.dmg

Linux, which compares them for you and shows OK:

sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing

With GPG, download SHA256SUMS.asc too: gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.asc SHA256SUMS checks that the list itself comes from Open Foris.

Install

Windows

  1. Double-click the .exe you downloaded. Don't choose Run as administrator: Collect Earth installs for your own account.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Choose your language, accept the licence and keep the folder it proposes, C:\OpenForis\CollectEarth, unless your organisation does not allow programs there.
  4. When it finishes, start Collect Earth from the icon on your desktop or from the Start menu.

If the antivirus stops it, or Collect Earth does not open, see If it does not install or start on Windows.

macOS

  1. Double-click the .dmg you downloaded, then the installer inside it.
  2. If macOS says it cannot check the installer, click Done (or OK). Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about the Collect Earth installer, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password.
  3. Follow the steps. Collect Earth goes into OpenForis/CollectEarth in your home folder, with a Collect Earth icon on your desktop.
  4. Start Collect Earth from that icon. On a Mac with an Apple chip, macOS may ask to install Rosetta the first time: click Install.

Linux

  1. Open a terminal in the folder you downloaded the installer to, make it executable and run it: 
    chmod +x collect-earth-installer-*-linux-x64.run
./collect-earth-installer-*-linux-x64.run
  2. Follow the steps. You don't need sudo: Collect Earth goes into ~/OpenForis/CollectEarth, with a launcher on your desktop.
  3. Start Collect Earth from that launcher (if your desktop asks, right-click it and choose Allow Launching), or run ~/OpenForis/CollectEarth/CollectEarth-launcher.run.

First start

  1. A small window shows each step while Collect Earth opens. The first start can take up to a minute.
  2. Collect Earth opens in your browser, inside Google Earth Web, with a demo project to try out. Google may ask you to accept its cookies or to sign in in that window; Collect Earth remembers it afterwards.
  3. Type your name when asked. It is saved with every plot you assess.

Collect Earth keeps running in the system tray (the menu bar on a Mac). From here, the guide for interpreters takes over: opening your project, assessing plots and joining a cloud project. Coordinators, see Running a cloud project.

If it does not install or start on Windows

Most problems on Windows come from security software or an office network, not from Collect Earth. These are the ones people report most often on the Open Foris forum, with what fixes them. Your data is not touched by any of these steps: it is in its own folder, %APPDATA%\CollectEarth, apart from the program.

“Windows protected your PC”

Windows SmartScreen shows this for programs it does not know yet, and the Collect Earth installer is not signed with a Microsoft certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. If there is no Run anyway, your organisation has turned it off: ask your IT to allow the installer. To be sure the file is the one Open Foris published, check the download first.

The antivirus stops the installer, or “Problem running post-install step”

Some antivirus programs, Kaspersky and Bitdefender among them, block the Collect Earth launcher while the installer sets it up, or move it to quarantine.

  1. Close the installer and delete the folder it left, C:\OpenForis\CollectEarth (or the folder you chose).
  2. Pause the antivirus while you install, or add an exception for that folder. If the antivirus is managed by your IT, ask them to do it.
  3. Install again with a double-click, not with Run as administrator.
  4. Turn the antivirus back on. If it now blocks Collect Earth when it starts, keep the exception for the folder.

Your organisation does not allow programs in C:\OpenForis

Some company rules let programs run only from certain folders. Install again and, when the installer proposes C:\OpenForis\CollectEarth, choose a folder you are allowed to use. Ask your IT which one.

It closes after the start window, or says it is already running

Collect Earth runs a small web server on your own computer, on port 8028, that the browser talks to. Start it while it is already running and the running one opens its window instead. If it says Collect Earth is already running and you don't see it:

  1. Open Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc), end any Collect Earth or Java task that is left (Java, javaw or OpenJDK Platform binary), and start Collect Earth again.
  2. If it still says so, another program is using port 8028. Quit Collect Earth, open %APPDATA%\CollectEarth\earth.properties in Notepad, change the line port=8028 to another number, for example port=8125 (add the line if it is not there), save the file and start Collect Earth.

The browser opens but Collect Earth does not appear in it

The browser reaches Collect Earth at an address on your own computer, http://127.0.0.1:8028/earth/…. Collect Earth listens only to your computer, not to the network, so Windows Firewall leaves it alone, but the web protection of some antivirus programs, or a firewall other than Windows', can still block it. In the antivirus, add 127.0.0.1 and localhost to the addresses its web protection leaves alone, and allow C:\OpenForis\CollectEarth\CollectEarth-launcher.exe (or ask your IT to).

Your office reaches the internet through a proxy

The imagery opens in your browser, which uses the office proxy by itself. Collect Earth's own connections (joining a cloud project and sending plots, checking for updates, getting the driver it uses to control the browser) use Java's proxy settings instead. Ask your IT for the proxy's address and port, and start Collect Earth from a small file that gives them to Java:

  1. Open Notepad and paste these lines, with your proxy's address and port in place of proxy.example.org and 8080 (and your installation folder, if you chose another): 
    @echo off
set "JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS=-Dhttp.proxyHost=proxy.example.org -Dhttp.proxyPort=8080 -Dhttps.proxyHost=proxy.example.org -Dhttps.proxyPort=8080"
start "" "C:\OpenForis\CollectEarth\CollectEarth-launcher.exe"
  2. Save it on your desktop as Collect Earth (proxy).bat, with Save as type set to All files.
  3. Start Collect Earth by double-clicking that file from now on.

Collect Earth's connections to your own computer (localhost, 127.0.0.1) never go through the proxy.

It does not start after installing over an older Collect Earth

The beta opens Collect Earth 1's data as it is. If it stops while starting, the log (see below) may say it could not open the database. Please send us that log. To get working again:

  1. Quit Collect Earth and open %APPDATA%\CollectEarth.
  2. Rename collectEarthDatabase.db to collectEarthDatabase-old.db. Don't delete it: it still holds your plots.
  3. Start Collect Earth, open your project, and import the data you exported before installing (Data Import/Export).

Finding the log

When Collect Earth runs, Help › Open Application Log File opens it. When it does not start, the log is earth_error.log in %APPDATA%\CollectEarth. That folder is hidden: type %APPDATA%\CollectEarth in the address bar of File Explorer and press Enter. Attach the log when you ask for help.

Installing on many computers

For a training room or a whole office, the installer can run with no questions, in the default folder or the one you give it:

collect-earth-installer-VERSION-windows.exe --mode unattended --prefix "D:\Apps\CollectEarth"

Each person's projects, data and settings stay in their own %APPDATA%\CollectEarth.

Something else? Search the Open Foris forum or ask there, saying which Windows and which antivirus you have, and attach the log.

Updates, going back and removing it

A new beta. Install it over the one you have, in the same way. Your projects, data and settings stay.

Your data. Collect Earth keeps your projects and plots in a folder of their own, apart from the program. Removing the program leaves that folder on your computer until you delete it yourself.

SystemYour data folder
Windows%APPDATA%\CollectEarth
macOS~/Library/Application Support/CollectEarth
Linux~/CollectEarth

Going back to Collect Earth 1. Install it again from the Open Foris download page. If it does not open your project as before, import the data you exported before installing the beta.

Removing it. On Windows, open Settings › Apps › Installed apps, then Collect Earth › Uninstall, or choose Uninstall Collect Earth in the Start menu. On macOS and Linux, run the uninstall program in OpenForis/CollectEarth in your home folder.
